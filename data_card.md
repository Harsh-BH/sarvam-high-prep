# Data card: Hindi (code-mixed) speech → English text, Part A pilot

DRAFT, 2026-10-14. Owner: W7. A personal learning project that uses the Inter IIT 15.0 Sarvam problem statement (`ps.md`) as its spec. Nothing is submitted or published. Statistics are generated by `uv run w7/stats.py <tag>` from the frozen release in S3, never typed by hand.

**Release described:** `release/pilot-v1` (S3 `sarvam-parta-114872709824`), manifest `release/pilot-v1/manifest.jsonl`, 621 records.

## 1. What a record is

- One single-speaker Hindi or Hinglish monologue clip, 24 kHz mono 16-bit WAV, ≤ 60 s (§3A, §4).
- The record holds a Hindi transcript in Devanagari only, one entry per sentence, with every word timed. It follows IndicVoices Level 1 with our deviations in SPEC §2.2: no Latin, numbers as words, fillers kept as Devanagari words.
- Each Hindi sentence has a fluent English translation (`en_text`, optionally `en_alt`), plus the provenance in `source` (§5.2).
- Format, field by field: `SPEC.md` §5. Every release passes `validate.py` with no violations (§8).

## 6. Reproducibility (§3A, §10 R7)

- `uv run pipeline/rebuild.py pilot-v1` rebuilds every WAV and the manifest from the bucket alone, and compares them byte for byte:
  - real records: re-cut from `raw/` with the pinned ffmpeg;
  - synthetic records: the archived take;
  - manifest: from the stage files frozen under `release/pilot-v1/inputs/`.
- Every paid or sampled call (Bedrock translations, judge, Bulbul) is archived write-once under `archive/`. The rebuild makes no API call.
- Last clean-machine rebuild: 2026-10-14, SageMaker ml.m5.large (`stages/rebuild/20261014T143034Z-c57d330`): **621/621 WAVs and the manifest byte-identical**.

## 7. Known limitations

- **Pilot size:** 3.3 h, against a 200 h cap. Real audio is IndicVoices only (5 states), so accent coverage is narrow.
- **Synthetic text:** a provisional probe set, not the W2 script corpus. Magpie's voices are English speakers reading Hindi. Their naturalness hasn't been listened to (W3).
- **Transcripts** are the corpus's L1 text after conversion. ASR consensus disagrees with about 0.5% of words (W4), and the 1 h human audit hasn't been run yet.
- **Code-mix rate** counts only English words the IndicVoices transcribers marked with a Latin gloss, so it's a lower bound.
- **Translations:** a record flagged critical after repair is kept and counted (§4 table). A record isn't dropped for a translation flag (W6).
- **Spelling (W4 question 1):** the dataset keeps IndicVoices' phonetic L1 spellings (`बोहोत`), about 2% of words.
